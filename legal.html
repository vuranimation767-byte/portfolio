Privacy policy

What this site is

A static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no advertising, no analytics and no payments on this site.

Who is responsible

Block Builder, the site operator, is responsible for the limited processing described below. You can reach me through the GitHub or Discord links on this page.

What is processed, and why

  • Hosting. GitHub Pages records standard request data — such as your IP address, browser, the page requested and the time — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Theme preference. Your light or dark choice is stored in your browser’s localStorage under the key theme. It never leaves your device and clearing site data removes it.
  • Videos. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play. When you do, the player loads from youtube-nocookie.com and Google may receive your IP address and device information and may set cookies or similar storage. The Google Privacy Policy applies from that point.
  • External links. GitHub, Discord and YouTube links only connect to those services when you follow them.
  • The admin editor. admin.html is used only by the site owner. Drafts and unlock state are kept in that browser’s storage and are not part of the public site.
  • View counts and comments. Both are currently switched off for this site. If either is enabled later, this policy will be updated before it goes live.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. After you press play, YouTube may set or read cookies and storage inside its embed; those are controlled by Google, not by this site.

Who else may receive data

GitHub (Microsoft) for hosting, and Google (YouTube) for video playback. If you contact me, GitHub or Discord will process that conversation. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. Some providers are based outside your country, including in the United States, and rely on their own transfer safeguards.

How long it is kept

Your theme preference stays until you clear it. Hosting logs are kept according to GitHub’s retention rules. Video data is controlled by Google. Messages are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, object to processing, request portability, or withdraw consent. Because this site stores almost nothing beyond your theme choice, the quickest route is usually to clear site data in your browser. For anything else, contact me and I will respond within the time the law requires. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is aimed at a general audience interested in Minecraft server development. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive personal information through Discord or GitHub messages.
